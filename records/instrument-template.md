## UCC Article 3 Requirements

### Negotiability Checklist (UCC § 3-104)
- [ ] Written and signed by the maker or drawer
- [ ] Contains an unconditional promise or order to pay
- [ ] States a fixed amount of money
- [ ] Payable on demand or at a definite time
- [ ] Payable to order or to bearer (unless check)
- [ ] Does not state any other undertaking by maker/drawer
