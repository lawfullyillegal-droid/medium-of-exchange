This dispute addresses multiple inaccuracies and unvalidated debts appearing on credit reports. The strategy is to:

1. **Demand Validation**: Request proof of debt and standing to collect per FDCPA
2. **Challenge Instrument Transfer**: Under UCC Article 3, demand proof of valid negotiable instrument and proper transfer
3. **Document Everything**: Maintain detailed records for potential litigation
4. **Escalate if Necessary**: File CFPB complaints and consider legal action for violations

- Original signed instrument (UCC § 3-104)
- Valid transfer creating negotiable instrument (UCC § 3-203)
- Holder in Due Course status (UCC § 3-302)

Without these, alleged creditors may lack standing to collect or report debt.
